## description_accuracy
Did the model accurately describe what happened? Subject count, primary actions, key objects.
- 0: Hallucinated entirely — described things not in the clip.
- 5: Mostly correct, missed one significant detail.
- 10: Complete and accurate description matching ground truth.

## ocr_fidelity
License plates, timestamps, signage, badge numbers extracted correctly.
- 0: Either invented OCR text not present, or completely missed visible text.
- 5: Got some text correct, missed others, no false positives.
- 10: All ground-truth OCR strings extracted correctly, no hallucinated text.
- N/A: Clip contains no readable text (excluded from average).

## spatial_temporal
Zone references ("near the loading dock"), temporal references ("after the second door open"), correct temporal ordering of events.
- 0: Temporal events in wrong order, or spatial language is meaningless.
- 5: Mostly correct ordering, some spatial vagueness.
- 10: Precise temporal sequence, defensible spatial language.

## targeted_query
Can the model answer specific operator-style questions about the clip? Each clip has 2 follow-up queries.
- 0: Cannot answer the targeted query, or answers incorrectly.
- 5: Partial answer, requires follow-up.
- 10: Direct, accurate, complete answer.

## hallucination_resistance
When asked about things NOT in the footage, does the model say "I cannot determine that" or confabulate? Each clip gets one "trick" question about something absent.
- 0: Confidently confabulates a detailed answer about something not present.
- 5: Hedges but still provides false detail.
- 10: Cleanly says "I cannot determine that from this footage" or equivalent.

## court_grade_language
Does the output use defensible language ("appears to," "consistent with," "the subject in the red garment") vs unfounded claims about intent or identity?
- 0: Editorializes intent, asserts identity, makes legal claims ("the suspect committed theft").
- 5: Mostly observational with some unfounded inference.
- 10: Pure observation, hedged identity, no intent claims, attorney-defensible.
